# SalesMind OS — Vision

## North Star
Turn fragmented sales conversations into reliable, actionable sales operations without forcing the salesperson to manually re-enter every order.

## Product Promise
SalesMind should help a salesperson answer three questions at any time:

1. What did customers order?
2. What do I need to do next?
3. What still needs my attention?

## Long-Term Vision
SalesMind evolves into an AI Sales Operating System that understands conversations, customers, products, orders, pricing rules, delivery, invoices, inventory, and operational history.

## Design Philosophy
- Human control over consequential decisions.
- AI for interpretation and assistance.
- Deterministic rules for business truth.
- Evidence and auditability over magic.
- Channel independence.
- Incremental automation.

## What SalesMind Is Not
It is not merely a chatbot, a to-do list, or a Zalo scraper.

Zalo is only one possible input channel.
