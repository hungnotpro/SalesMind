# SalesMind OS — User Personas

## Primary: Sales Operator
Receives orders through chat, interprets shorthand, coordinates delivery and invoices, and needs a reliable daily task list.

Needs:
- fast processing
- minimal manual entry
- clear review queue
- customer history
- daily workload

## Secondary: Sales Manager
Needs visibility into orders, unresolved items, task status, and team workload.

## Secondary: Accounting Operator
Needs reliable invoice requirements and traceable order evidence. Does not want AI to invent accounting values.

## Secondary: Delivery Operator
Needs clear delivery tasks, customer address, phone, requested delivery date, and order reference.

## Future: Administrator
Configures products, aliases, business rules, users, integrations, and permissions.
